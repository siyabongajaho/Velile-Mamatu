About

A multidisciplinary perspective

I support parents and caregivers of neurodivergent children, particularly those with autism and ADHD, as well as learning disorders. Families may be navigating a new diagnosis, communication or behavioural challenges, learning needs, school concerns, or caregiver stress.

As a qualified Speech-Language Therapist and Audiologist with a BA Honours in Psychology and PGCE registration, I bring a multidisciplinary perspective to my work. My approach is neuro-affirming, family-centred and collaborative, helping parents better understand their child, strengthen connection, advocate for their needs, and feel supported in their own journey.

I provide neuro-affirming support around communication, behaviour, learning and emotional wellbeing, and offer a safe, non-judgmental space to talk, understand your child and find practical ways forward together. Together, we can build understanding, practical strategies, stronger connection, and confidence in advocating for your child's needs.
